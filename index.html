A note from me

I paint to reconnect with myself.

A blank canvas has always been nearby—through childhood, through moving, through short mornings with coffee when I could already see color, shape, and texture. For a long time it stayed personal: something I returned to when I had time and needed stillness. Then my home filled with paintings. Friends paused. People who look carefully noticed. That is when I decided to take it seriously.

I work from feeling—human movement, small moments, the quiet momentum between them. I want the work to hold beauty, and to light a room without shouting. I prefer to revive what already exists: reclaimed materials, surfaces given a second life, techniques found by curiosity rather than waste. Playfulness matters too—friends bring that energy; imagination does the rest.

My studio is a separate space next to the house, where I can try, fail, and return. Nature and the figure meet there. So do texture, color, and the simple wish to make something honest.

I am Andreea. I live in Dubai. Everything I make is signed SARVIIII.

The Studio

My studio is a separate space next to the house—unconventional, quiet, and mine.

It is not a showroom. It is where I test color, texture, and form; where reclaimed materials get a second life; where nature and the figure meet on canvas. I come here to play as much as to focus: to try, to revise, to let feeling become surface.

This is where imagined shapes find their place—and where SARVIIII is made.
